## YouTube (channel description)

Measured Past makes 30-second films about history and archaeology. Each one
answers a single question, such as how much of Stonehenge is still standing,
using laser scans, published surveys and scans of original documents. What
survives is shown as it is; what we infer is marked as ours. Sources for every
film are listed in its description. Narration is a synthetic voice.
