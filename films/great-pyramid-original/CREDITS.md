# Credits: What did the Great Pyramid originally look like?

## On screen (under the spoken title, and over the closing pull-back)

> Survey: Petrie 1883, pl. VIII and p. 43 · Synthetic voice

## For the post description

The white model is built from the level of every course as surveyed by W. M. Flinders Petrie in 1880-82 (The Pyramids and Temples of Gizeh, London 1883, Plate VIII; public domain, scans from Cornell University Library via the Internet Archive). The casing and apex in light are from his base and face angle (p. 43). The picture is a model drawn from survey data, not footage. Narration is a synthetic voice (Kokoro). Full sources: FACTS.md.

## Voice, type and software

- Narration: Kokoro-82M by hexgrad (Apache-2.0), voice `bm_fable`, British English, speed 1.05, run with kokoro-onnx (MIT). A synthetic voice; it imitates no real person.
- Sound: room tone and cues are generated by code (`engine/sound.py`). No samples or music.
- Type: Inter and Inter Display, Copyright (c) 2016 The Inter Project Authors (SIL Open Font License 1.1); JetBrains Mono, Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
- Picture: drawn with moderngl (OpenGL) and Pillow; encoded with PyAV (FFmpeg, libx264).
