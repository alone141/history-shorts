## Not yet confirmed

- Whether the Metropolitan Museum or the Internet Archive asks for its credit line as a requirement or a courtesy. The Met record pages answered 429 to curl, so the CC0 status was read from the Met collection API's flag, and the holders' policy pages were not checked.
- The name of the 1651 author, which the notes spell three ways (Joseph-Marie Suarès, Joseph Marie Suares, Giuseppe Maria Suarès). The credit line above uses "Suaresius" in the post only.
- The 1651 date and place. It is the archive's record date; the title-page imprint was not read, and a Lyon reprint is dated 1652.
