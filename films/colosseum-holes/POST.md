## Description (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube)

Most of the Colosseum's holes are where people dug out the iron clamps that tied its stone blocks together, after Rome fell. Middleton gave that as the explanation in 1892. One estimate puts the metal used to build it at about 300 tons.

Sources: Middleton, The Remains of Ancient Rome (1892); Suaresius, Diatriba de foraminibus lapidum (1651); Art of Making, King's College London; Piranesi (1776) and St. John (1856), Metropolitan Museum of Art, CC0.
