## Description (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube)

Only 17 of the 30 stones in Stonehenge's outer circle still stand, and engineers re-set or secured 13 of those last century. The white model is what survives today. The light is what we infer was there.

Did you expect more, or less?

Sources: plan of the stones after Nash et al. 2021, PLoS ONE 16(8): e0254760, Fig 1B (CC BY 4.0), outlines traced and given measured heights. Positions of missing stones after Darvill 2022, Antiquity 96: 319-335 (CC BY 4.0). Heights from the Environment Agency National LIDAR Programme, © Environment Agency copyright and/or database right 2022, Open Government Licence v3.0.
