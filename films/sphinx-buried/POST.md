## Description (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube)

Selim Hassan wrote in 1949 that after the 4th century AD the Great Sphinx was buried up to its neck and stayed that way until recent times. The 1,500 years is our count from that sentence; he gives no figure. Norden drew it that way in 1737, and a photograph from 1851-52 shows the head, neck and upper chest above the sand. Would you have recognised it?
