## Voice, type and software

- Narration: Kokoro-82M by hexgrad (Apache-2.0), voice `bm_fable`, British English, speed 1.05, run with kokoro-onnx (MIT). A synthetic voice; it imitates no real person. These settings are copied from the Stonehenge credits, as the brief asked.
- Sound: room tone and cues are generated by code (`engine/sound.py`). No samples or music. The film has two tick cues.
- Type: Inter and Inter Display, Copyright (c) 2016 The Inter Project Authors (SIL Open Font License 1.1); JetBrains Mono, Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
- Picture: drawn with Pillow; encoded with PyAV (FFmpeg, libx264).
